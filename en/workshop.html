Workshop mode

Four levels of participation, three warning signs and four check questions, one per slide. Arrow keys or the space bar change slides, Esc ends the show.

To the worksheet

Youth influence has to be more than a picture in a campaign.

Four levels of participation · 1/4

Decoration

Young people are used to create feeling, energy or credibility.

Four levels of participation · 2/4

Consultation

Young people may give their views, but the framework is already decided.

Four levels of participation · 3/4

Co-creation

Young people are involved early and influence goals, content and priorities.

Four levels of participation · 4/4

Decision-making power

Young people have a mandate, resources and the ability to stop or change decisions.

Three warning signs · 01

Young people are seen but do not decide

They appear in pictures, panel discussions and campaigns but have no influence over decisions, budget and strategy.

Three warning signs · 02

Radical language, unchanged structure

The organisation talks about the future and participation, but the same people still hold all the power.

Three warning signs · 03

Engagement becomes branding

Young people's causes are packaged as image without long-term support for the work already under way.

Four check questions · 1/4

Timing

Were young people involved before the problem, the goal and the solution were decided?

Four check questions · 2/4

Mandate

Are there decisions young people can actually influence, or may they only give their views?

Four check questions · 3/4

Resources

Do young people get pay, knowledge, time and support to take part on reasonable terms?

Four check questions · 4/4

Feedback

Does the organisation show what changed after young people's input, and what did not?
