Method

A simpler way to examine young people's influence.

The site is built as a clear mini-resource: less slogan, more check questions, examples and usable criteria.

Purpose

Why the site exists

Many organisations want to show that they listen to young people. That is good when it is true, but problematic when it is mostly communication. This site helps the visitor ask better questions.

It is not built to cast suspicion on every campaign with young faces. It is built to tell actual participation apart from symbolic presence.

Review framework

Four questions that reveal the level

Timing

Were young people involved before the problem, the goal and the solution were decided?

Mandate

Are there decisions young people can actually influence, or may they only give their views?

Resources

Do young people get pay, knowledge, time and support to take part on reasonable terms?

Feedback

Does the organisation show what changed after young people's input, and what did not?

Principle

A simple rule of thumb

If young people are used for legitimacy, they should also get legitimate opportunities to influence. Otherwise the representation is mostly decoration.
