Gather the group

Each participant takes the self-assessment on their own phone and gets a short code. Paste the codes here, one participant per line, ideally with a role after the code. Nothing is sent anywhere: everything is computed in this browser.

For example: 2101-13 board. A role can be young participant, staff, board or your own text. The code is shown below the result in the self-assessment.

      How the disagreement is calculated

      Answers count as no 0, partly 1 and yes 2. Spread is the standard deviation: 0 means everyone answered the same, 1 that the group is split between no and yes. Gap between roles is the distance between the highest and the lowest role average. The discussion order sorts by spread plus gap between roles.
